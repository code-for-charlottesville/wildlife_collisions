Setting Up Your Animal Intake Web Form

Quickstart Guide

Step 1:

Make sure you're signed in to the primary Google account you wish to connect the form with. Then create a copy of the template Google Form by clicking "Copy Google Form" below. On the opened page click "Make a copy".

Step 2:

Feel free to change the form name, but do not edit or delete any of the required questions. Next navigate to the Responses tab and click "Link to Sheets" to create a new spreadsheet.

Step 3:

Return to the Google Form and under more options select "Get pre-filled link". Then enter dummy responses into only required question (marked with a red asterisks). Do not provide responses for non-required questions.

Note the responses do not need be real patient data, but try to provide a realistic example. Once you have entered responses into each of the form's required fields, click "Get link" at the bottom of the form. Then click "COPY LINK" to copy the link to your clipboard.

Step 4:

Paste the link from Step 3 into the input below.
