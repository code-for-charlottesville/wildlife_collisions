Setting up your digital animal intake form

Quickstart

Step 1:

Make sure you're signed in to the primary Google account you wish to connect the form with.


Step 2:

Create a copy of the underlying Google Form by clicking "Copy Google Form" below. On the opened page click "Make a copy".


Step 3:

Once you have copied the form, follow the instructions below to create the underlying spreadsheet. (Steps 3.1 - 3.3)

Optionally, if you would like to rename the form, now is a good time to do so. To do so, click into the text input field at the top left-hand corner of the site.

Step 3.1

Upon copying the form, navigate to the Responses tab at the top of the page.

Step 3.2

Select Link to Sheets.

Step 3.3

Ensuring "Create a new spreadsheet" is selected, click Create. Doing so should open that spreadsheet

Optionally, if you would like to add any addtional questions, make sure they are not Required. Adding a required question is not supported with this template.

Step 4:

Return to the Google Form and proceed with steps 4.1 - 4.3 below.

Step 4.1

Select the three vertical dots (⋮) in the upper right corner of the site .

Step 4.2

From the dropdown, choose "Get pre-filled link".

Step 4.3

Enter data into question entries with red asterisks next to their names.
The data only needs to be a valid entry, the content doesn't matter.
For example, entering "a" into each short answer box is entirely acceptable,
but entering 88:99 AM into a time field is not.

When entering data, make sure you fill in every required field and no other fields.
Once you have entered data into each of the form's required fields, click the button labeled "Get link".

Step 5:

Paste the link from Step 4 into the input below.
