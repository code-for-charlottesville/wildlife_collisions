Setting Up Your Digital Animal Intake Form

Quickstart Guide

Step 1:

Make sure you're signed in to the primary Google account you wish to connect the form with.


Step 2:

Create a copy of the underlying Google Form by clicking "Copy Google Form" below. On the opened page click "Make a copy".


Step 3:

Feel free to change the form name, but do not edit or delete any of the required questions. If you add any addtional questions, make sure they are not required questions (adding a required question is not supported with this template). Then follow steps 3.1 - 3.3 below to create the underlying spreadsheet.

Step 3.1

Upon copying the form, navigate to the Responses tab at the top of the page.

Step 3.2

Click "Link to Sheets".

Step 3.3

Make sure "Create a new spreadsheet" is selected, then click Create.

Step 4:

Return to the Google Form and proceed with steps 4.1 - 4.3 below to generate the form prefill link.

Step 4.1

Select the three vertical dots (⋮) in the upper right corner of the site .

Step 4.2

From the dropdown, choose "Get pre-filled link".

Step 4.3

Enter dummy responses into each required question only (marked with a red asterisks). Do not provide responses for non-required questions.

Note, in step 4.3 the responses do not need be real patient data, but try to provide a realistic example. Once you have entered responses into each of the form's required fields, click "Get link" at the bottom of the form. Then click "COPY LINK" to copy the link to your clipboard.

Step 5:

Paste the link from Step 4 into the input below.
